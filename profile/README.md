We make small, focused apps for macOS.

### [boringBar](https://boringbar.app)
A taskbar for macOS. Keeps your windows organized by desktop, with previews, pinned apps and one-click desktop switching.

### [Chest](https://chest.boringbar.app)
A free, open-source menu bar manager for macOS. Hide the menu bar icons you don't need, right in the menu bar or in a drawer. [Source](https://github.com/boringbar/chest)
